## What the corpus contains

The released `.sbcsr` corpus contains **only MeSH® descriptor indices** (integer
feature ids) in compressed-sparse-row form. It contains **no PMIDs, no titles, and no
abstracts**. NLM's note that some PubMed/MEDLINE abstracts may be protected by
copyright therefore does not apply to any material distributed here.
